Progress

See your strength climb, week by week.

Every workout you save adds to your progress: how much you lifted, how long you trained, and which muscles did the work.

  • This week at a glance
  • Any period, any metric
  • Muscle breakdown
  • Every session
  • Heart rate, with Apple Watch
  • Your way

This week at a glance

The Progress tab opens on this week.

  • Volume and workout time, with the trend against last week.
  • A calendar of the days you trained, your total sessions and your week streak.
  • Sets per muscle group this week.

Any period, any metric

Open a card to see it over time.

  • 7 days, 14 days, 1 month, 12 months or All, with a wheel to go back in time.
  • A bar per day, month or year, with the average marked.
  • Compare with… puts another period beside it.

Muscle breakdown

Where your sets went.

  • A donut of sets per muscle group, with a table.
  • Tap a slice to pop it out.

Every session

Your history, one tap away.

  • Tap a day to open its workout: edit sets, notes, supersets, the start time and the length, or delete it.
  • Tap an empty day to log a workout after the fact.
  • Every month: a year of small calendars, back to your first workout.

Heart rate, with Apple Watch

Workouts from the watch bring their heart rate.

  • Average and max heart rate, and active calories.
  • A heart-rate graph over the workout, with high-effort zones highlighted.

Your way

Two settings shape what you see.

  • Weeks start on Sunday or Monday.
  • Weights in lbs or kg.
