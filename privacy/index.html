Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

8GYM works without an account. Your workouts stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and back up to your own iCloud Drive if you turn that on.

The app

Plans, exercises, workouts, photos and settings are stored on your device. The app has no servers of its own and no analytics, and the developer can’t see your data. Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

The iPhone and Apple Watch app sync with each other directly. Sharing a plan or exercises makes a code you send yourself, through any app you choose.

iCloud backups

Backups go to your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account. You can also export a backup file or CSV and keep it wherever you like.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, 8GYM saves each workout’s type, time and duration, and for watch workouts heart rate, active calories and effort. It reads your birth date to set heart-rate zones. Health data stays in Health and is never used for advertising.

Tips

Tips are in-app purchases handled by Apple. The developer doesn’t receive your payment details.

This website

If you accept cookies, this site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which ads bring people here. Google receives your browser and device details, an approximate location and the pages you view. If you decline, Analytics doesn’t load.

The library builder runs in your browser. The lists and files you put into it are never uploaded or sent to Analytics.
